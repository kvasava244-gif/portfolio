Thesis & projects

IN PLAIN TERMS

Dams hold back water rivers need to stay healthy. I compared 32 rivers before and after their dams were built, using six methods — and found dams, not weather, caused 94% of the change.

Environmental flow assessment of regulated rivers in peninsular India
32 STATIONS · PRE/POST-DAM COMPARISON · 2024–2026

M.Tech thesis, IIT Gandhinagar. Compared pre- vs. post-dam streamflow across six methods and attributed the change to climate vs. dam operation using the Budyko–Choudhury–Yang framework.

T30 / T40RVAFDCASmakhtinVMFBudyko–Choudhury–Yang
Skills used: statistical hydrology · Python/R modelling · attribution analysis
FIG. 01Study area — 32 gauging stations and dams across peninsular India.
FIG. 02Pre-dam e-flow compliance, six methods.
FIG. 03Post-dam — visibly paler, meaning less water reaching the river.
FIG. 04Every month falls post-dam — July drops the most.

Also used microwave remote sensing, drone acquisition and GIS for geospatial analysis, with Claude Cowork Pro and Google Antigravity throughout.

Drone survey & 3D modelling of IIT Gandhinagar campus
GROUP PROJECT, 5 MEMBERS · DEPT. OF EARTH SCIENCES, IIT GANDHINAGAR · 2025

Flew a drone over the campus and took 1,175 overlapping photos, using GPS ground markers to keep the measurements accurate to the centimetre. Then used software to turn those photos into a 3D model, a height map, and a stitched-together aerial photo of the whole area.
